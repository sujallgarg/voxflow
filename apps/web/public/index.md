# VoxFlow — Multilingual AI Voice Typing & Meeting Summaries for Web

> Website: https://voxflowai.devs.surf/
> Description: Fast, privacy-conscious multilingual voice typing and structured AI meeting summaries for your browser.

## Core Capabilities
- **Multilingual AI Voice Typing**: Speak in 50+ languages with automatic punctuation, filler word cleanup, and context formatting (Slack, Email, Markdown).
- **AI Meeting Summaries**: Record online meetings or tab audio to automatically generate executive summaries, key discussion points, decisions, and action items.
- **Chrome Extension & Web Companion**: Works seamlessly anywhere you write on the web.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VoxFlow?

VoxFlow is a multilingual, context-aware AI voice typing tool that converts natural speech into clear, structured, polished text in your preferred language across web applications.

Which languages does it support?

VoxFlow supports major global languages (including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese) and natively handles conversational code-switching like Hinglish or Spanglish.

Is a credit card required to get started?

No. You can sign up and start using the Free plan immediately without entering any credit card or billing information.

Does VoxFlow work with browser-based applications?

Yes. VoxFlow works directly across web applications including Gmail, Slack, Google Docs, Notion, web forms, and customer support desks.
